## Visual research

The user selected [Wispr Flow](https://wisprflow.ai/) as a visual reference.

The page uses cream, ink, deep teal, and lavender.
The page pairs Figtree body text with EB Garamond headings.
Its layout uses large serif headlines, italic accents, outlined controls, and broad section curves.

Ruruma uses those general design principles with original copy and composition.
Ruruma does not reproduce Wispr Flow's logo, product media, testimonials, customer logos, or outcome claims.
The reference site's certifications do not apply to Ruruma.

## Assumptions and limits

The first audience is founders and operations leaders at small and medium businesses.
The English site can serve worldwide enquiries.
Germany and India are the first market assumptions, not exclusive service regions.
Each proposed AI integration needs discovery, data access, a written scope, and a baseline.
Regulated use cases need a separate assessment before implementation.
No customer testimonials or guaranteed revenue figures support this release.
