      A few good questions.
      Where do we start? 
      With one workflow and a clear problem. We review the data, current tools, and baseline together, then agree on a discovery or pilot scope.
      Can you work with our existing tools? 
      That is the starting point. We check available APIs, permissions, and data constraints before we commit to an integration. Provider fees and access requirements form part of the scope.
      Do you guarantee savings or more revenue? 
      No. We agree on measures and test against a baseline. Results depend on the workflow, data, adoption, and operating conditions. The aim is evidence you can use to decide the next step.
      How does pricing work? 
      Each engagement has an agreed scope, deliverables, and fee before work starts. Discovery, pilot development, integration, and ongoing improvement can be separate stages. Third-party service costs need your approval.
      What happens to our data? 
      This website does not collect your brief on a server. For a project, we agree on data access, retention, provider choices, and human review before your business data enters a system.
