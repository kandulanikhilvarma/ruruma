Privacy information · Updated 22 October 2026

Ruruma is the AI engineering brand of Nikhilvarma Kandula. Contact the founder at kandulanikhilvarma@gmail.com for privacy questions.

The project brief

The brief tool runs in your browser. It does not submit your entries to a Ruruma server, an AI model, or a form provider. Do not enter passwords or confidential customer data.

The tool creates a suggested project scope from a local set of service examples. It does not make an AI API call.

Drafts on your device

Your draft stays in memory unless you select “Save my draft on this device.” If you select that option, the website stores the form fields in your browser’s local storage.

The website checks the draft’s age on your next visit. It removes drafts older than 30 days when the tool loads. Browser storage is not encrypted by this website. Avoid this option on shared devices.

Clear draft removes the saved record and resets the form. Clear site data in your browser settings if the website cannot remove the record.

Email and file exports

Download brief creates a text file on your device. Copy brief text puts the brief on your clipboard if your browser permits it.

Open email draft passes the brief to your configured email application through a mailto link. Your email application and operating system can process that content. Review the draft before you send it.

If you send an email, the founder receives your address and message through Gmail. The founder uses that information to respond and discuss the potential project. Email records can remain in that account until they are no longer needed for the conversation or project.

You can ask to correct or delete your email correspondence. Applicable legal record duties can affect deletion. Contact the founder at the address above.

Hosting and external links

The website does not add analytics, advertising trackers, or third-party embeds. Fonts and brand assets load from this website.

The hosting provider can process request information, such as your IP address and browser details, to deliver and secure the website. The current public version uses OpenAI Sites. Its provider policies apply to that processing.

GitHub, Kandula Studio, LinkedIn, and other external destinations have their own privacy practices. The website only contacts them when you follow a link.

Accessibility

The website uses semantic headings, visible keyboard focus, labelled form fields, and clear validation errors. Navigation, contact links, and service descriptions work without an account.

The site supports reduced motion. The brief tool and industry explorer need JavaScript. Direct email contact remains available without JavaScript.

If a control prevents you from completing a task, email the founder. Include the page, your browser, and the step that failed. You can also send a project brief directly by email.
