# Ruruma

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated by the user: static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript modules.
Node.js scripts build the site and run tests.
The browser needs no framework runtime.
Vercel is the preferred host, subject to account access and plan rules.

## Users

Assumption: founders and operations leaders at small and medium businesses are the first audience.
The English website serves visitors worldwide.
Germany and India are useful first markets, not exclusive service regions.

## Product Purpose

Ruruma is the AI engineering brand of Nikhilvarma Kandula.
The brand offers AI systems that target revenue, process efficiency, and lower operating costs.
Visitors can explore services, review founder work, and prepare a project brief.
Visitors can download that brief or open it in their email application.

## Positioning

Start with one business workflow and a measurable baseline.
Build a bounded pilot with human review and cost limits.
Measure results before wider deployment.

## Operating Context

Ruruma is a public brand website, not a client portal.
Discovery defines integrations, data access, delivery scope, and price.
The first release does not connect to visitor business systems.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Service exploration by business outcome and industry.
- Founder portfolio with source links.
- Project brief with validation and a text export.
- Optional browser-local draft storage, with explicit visitor choice and a clear action.
- Direct email contact at kandulanikhilvarma@gmail.com.

- No accounts, payment collection, analytics, or server intake in release one.
- No automatic email delivery or claim that the website sent a message.
- Free services only unless the user approves a paid commitment.
- Apache-2.0 for project code.

## Brand Commitments

Use Ruruma as the name.
The user's final instruction resolves an earlier Enamamula reference.
Use the supplied logo from download.svg.
Use Wispr Flow as design inspiration, with original Ruruma content and composition.
The intended custom domain is ruruma.me.

## Evidence on Hand

The founder's public portfolio and 25 public repositories support the work section.
Witness, Ganymede, and FirstChair are founder projects, not Ruruma customer engagements.
Ganymede's reported lift is a backtest result.
FirstChair is a pre-launch MVP.
No verified Ruruma customer testimonials or revenue outcomes are available.

## Product Principles

- Make the business outcome clear before the technology.
- Name the measure and the limits of each pilot.
- Keep facts separate from examples.
- Keep visitor drafts under visitor control.
- Make every visible control useful.

## Accessibility and Inclusion

Use semantic HTML and visible keyboard focus.
Support reduced motion, mobile layouts, and accessible form errors.
Maintain readable contrast and touch targets of at least 44 pixels.

## Release acceptance

Visitors can find services, industry examples, founder evidence, and contact details.
The brief tool validates input and creates a complete, downloadable brief.
Draft recovery works only after the visitor selects save on this device.
Visitors can clear the saved draft.

The site works without a backend or secret keys.
Navigation and direct email remain available without JavaScript.
Local tests and desktop and mobile browser checks must pass before publication.

## Roadmap

Add server intake only after the owner selects an email provider and a data retention policy.
Add client-approved case studies when verified evidence exists.
Add a secure client portal only when actual projects need it.
Add scheduling only after the founder supplies a real calendar.
